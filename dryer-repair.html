Dryer repair FAQ

Useful answers before you call.

Why is my dryer running but not heating?
A dryer can run without producing proper heat for several reasons. An in-home diagnosis helps identify the source before a repair is recommended.
Why is my dryer taking too long to dry clothes?
Long drying times can be related to heating, airflow, sensing, or other operating issues. The correct repair depends on the actual cause.
Do you provide dryer repair in the GTA?
