Washer & dryer repair FAQ

Useful answers before you call.

Why is my washer not draining or spinning?
Washer drain and spin problems can have several possible causes. An in-home diagnosis helps identify what is stopping the cycle from completing normally before a repair is recommended.
Why is my washer leaking?
A washer leak can come from different points in the appliance or its operation. The correct repair depends on where the water is coming from and when the leak occurs.
Why is my dryer running but not heating?
A dryer can run without producing proper heat for several reasons. An in-home diagnosis helps identify the source before a repair is recommended.
