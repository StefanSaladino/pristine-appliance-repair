Privacy

Privacy Policy.

This policy explains what information Pristine Appliance Repair may collect through this website, why it is used, and the choices available when analytics and advertising technologies are enabled.

Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. Information you provide

When you send a repair request, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, postal code, appliance type, and a description of the appliance problem. If you contact Pristine by phone or another method, you may also provide information needed to respond to your service request.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, health information, or other sensitive information through the website form.

2. Website and device information

When analytics or advertising technologies are enabled, the website and its service providers may process information such as browser and device type, IP address or approximate location, pages viewed, referring pages, interactions with the site, advertising click information, and cookie or similar identifiers.

Necessary browser storage may also be used to remember your privacy preferences and support basic website operation.

3. How information is used

  • To receive, understand, and respond to appliance repair requests.
  • To communicate about a requested service.
  • To maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website.
  • With the appropriate consent, to understand website traffic and performance.
  • With the appropriate consent, to measure Google Ads activity and advertising performance.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, security, or fraud-prevention obligations where applicable.

4. Google Analytics and Google Ads

Pristine Appliance Repair plans to use Google Analytics and Google Ads after launch for website measurement, advertising measurement, and campaign improvement. Google technologies may use cookies or similar identifiers and may receive information about visits and ad interactions.

The site uses Google Consent Mode v2-compatible privacy controls. Analytics storage and advertising-related consent signals default to denied until you make a choice. If you reject non-essential technologies, those consent signals remain denied. If you later change your mind, use .

Google explains its privacy practices at Google Privacy Policy and provides additional information about business data practices at Google Business Data Responsibility.

5. Cookies, local storage, and your choices

Necessary

Necessary storage supports essential website functions and remembers your privacy choice. It cannot be switched off through the privacy panel because the preference itself needs to be remembered.

Analytics

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics or similar measurement tools may store or read identifiers used to understand visits, interactions, and site performance.

Advertising

If you allow advertising, Google Ads-related technologies may use storage or data for advertising measurement and, where enabled, advertising personalization.

Your consent choice is stored in your browser. Clearing browser storage may cause the privacy banner to appear again.

6. Service providers and disclosure

Information may be processed by companies that provide website hosting, form processing, analytics, advertising measurement, security, or other technical services on behalf of Pristine Appliance Repair. These providers may process information in Canada or other countries and are subject to their own legal obligations and contractual terms.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights or safety, investigate misuse, or support a business transaction where permitted by law.

7. Retention and safeguards

Personal information should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including service follow-up, recordkeeping, security, and legal requirements. Retention periods for analytics and advertising data may also depend on the settings of the relevant service provider.

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards should be used to protect information. No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your privacy choices and access

You can accept, reject, or later change optional analytics and advertising choices through the site’s privacy controls. Depending on applicable Canadian privacy law and your circumstances, you may also have rights to ask about personal information held about you, request access or correction, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing.

To make a privacy request, use the Contact page or call Pristine Appliance Repair using the phone action on this website. Enough information may be requested to verify and respond to the request.

9. Children’s privacy

This website is intended for people seeking household appliance repair services and is not directed to children. Do not submit personal information about a child unless it is necessary and lawful to do so.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as website tools, advertising or analytics practices, service providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Pristine Appliance Repair through the website contact page or by using the Call Now action on this site.
