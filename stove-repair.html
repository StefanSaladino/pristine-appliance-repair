Stove repair FAQ

Useful answers before you call.

Why is my stove burner not heating properly?
A burner that does not heat properly can have more than one possible cause. An in-home diagnosis helps identify the issue before a repair is recommended.
Why is my stove temperature inconsistent?
Inconsistent heat can relate to the way the appliance is producing, regulating, or responding to temperature. The correct repair depends on the actual source of the symptom.
Do you provide stove repair in the GTA?
Current service areas include Mississauga, Vaughan, Etobicoke, Thornhill, Scarborough, North York, and East York.
