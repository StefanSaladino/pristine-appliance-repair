Dishwasher repair FAQ

Useful answers before you call.

Why is my dishwasher not draining?
A dishwasher that won’t drain can have several possible causes. A diagnosis is needed to identify the actual issue before the repair is recommended.
Why are my dishes still dirty after a cycle?
Poor cleaning can be related to water movement, drainage, cycle operation, or other appliance issues. The correct repair depends on what is causing the symptom.
